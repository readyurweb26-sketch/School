About Us

A nurturing ICSE school rooted in Majlishpur, Kaliyaganj

Urmila Academy (ICSE) is a private English medium institution dedicated to giving young learners — from Nursery through Class V — a strong academic foundation alongside warm, personal care.

Located in Majlishpur, Kaliyaganj, West Bengal, our campus is built around one simple belief: children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and encouraged to be curious. We follow the ICSE curriculum while making space for music, movement, and expression in everyday school life.
