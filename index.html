About Us

A nurturing ICSE school rooted in Majlishpur, Kaliyaganj

Urmila Academy (ICSE) is a private English & Bengali medium institution dedicated to giving young learners — from Nursery through Class V — a strong academic foundation alongside warm, personal care.

Located in Majlishpur, Kaliyaganj, West Bengal, our campus is built around one simple belief: children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and encouraged to be curious. We follow the ICSE curriculum while making space for music, movement, and expression in everyday school life.

As one of the ICSE-approved English & Bengali medium schools in Kaliyaganj, Urmila Academy is built to give every child — from their very first day in Nursery through Class V — good teachers, structured guidance, and a clear foundation for the years ahead. Families from Kaliyaganj, Raiganj, Kushmandi and Hemtabad choose us for exactly this reason: a school that treats each child's future as seriously as their present.
